## F4 (blocker): a full withdrawal is invisible when Upbit omits the account row

`_bootstrap` and `_record_balance_evidence` only look at markets present in `accounts()`. Upbit does not guarantee a row for a currency with zero balance and zero locked. That causes two problems:

- **Stale residual:** After a release with residual `1`, a full withdrawal that drops the row leaves `external_quantities['KRW-A']='1'` unchanged. A later re-deposit of the same `1` then matches the stale baseline, so the market is not re-protected. This is the withdrawal class F2 was meant to cover. `test_withdrawal_deficit_reprotects...` only covers the case where the row survives.
- **Ledger deficit:** A ledger market with no account row is never evaluated.

**Minimal fix** in `manual_protection.py`, inside `scan`, immediately after the `accounts()` try block:

```python
        doc=self.policy.read()
        tracked=set(doc.get('bootstrap',{}))|{normalize(m) for m in state.get('positions',{})}
        for m in ({market} if market else tracked)-set(inventory): inventory[m]=(Decimal(0),Decimal(0))
```

Why this is enough:
- Because of `qty<=EPS`, zero rows never create a new bootstrap.
- The evidence step then records `0 - owned`.
- A re-deposit then differs from that baseline and re-protects.

**Required regression** in `DetectionTests`:

```python
    def test_full_withdrawal_with_missing_account_row_resets_residual(self):
        self.state['positions']={};self.detector.scan(self.state)
        self.policy.decide('A',False,expected=self.policy.revision('A'))
        self.api.rows=[];self.detector.scan(self.state)
        self.assertEqual(self.policy.read()['external_quantities']['KRW-A'],'0')
        self.api.rows=[{'currency':'A','balance':'1','locked':'0'}];self.detector.scan(self.state)
        self.assertIn('KRW-A',effective(self.policy.read()))
```
