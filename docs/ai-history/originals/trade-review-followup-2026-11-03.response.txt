## Non-blocking notes (fix later if convenient)

- **`stat()` is outside the `try`** in `debate_context`. A file vanishing between `exists()` and `stat()` raises `FileNotFoundError`. Similarly, a top-level non-dict JSON raises an uncaught `AttributeError` on `.get`. The broker's outer `except Exception` contains both, so the claim "catches OSError too" is only true at the broker layer. Moving `stat()` inside the `try` and adding `AttributeError` would make the function self-contained.
- **`os.replace` can fail on Windows** with `PermissionError` while a reader has `latest.json` open. The watcher catches it, writes an `ok:false` health file, and retries in 15 s. That is self-healing, but expect occasional transient health failures in the logs.
- **One malformed closed journal line stops the watcher permanently.** Every cycle raises, so `latest.json` goes stale and the debate correctly reports "갱신 지연". This is fail-closed, which is right, but someone needs to watch `health.json` or it can stay stuck indefinitely.
- **Zero-qty buy rows with `fee > 0` are not counted in `unassigned_fee_rows`.** Their fee is added to `pos['cost']` and silently carried into the next recorded lot's basis. This is rare, but it is a small misattribution. Counting them like the sell-side late fees would be more consistent.
- **Identical duplicate journal lines share a fingerprint.** They are archived once but counted twice in the summary. This is fine if order identifiers are always present in rows; just be aware of it.
- **The "new fills since the opponent's last acknowledged version" instruction is hard to follow precisely.** The hash isn't diffable, so models must compare by `index`/`time` across turns. Expect approximate rather than exact incremental analysis.
